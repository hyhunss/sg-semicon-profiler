# US Prospect Discovery: OneSystems Technologies

## 1. Search Strategy Summary
* Source capability profile: `data/onesystems_technologies_capabilities.md`
* Prospect scope: US semiconductor and semiconductor-adjacent facilities, including fabs, advanced packaging, materials, R&D/pilot-line facilities, and EPC/channel routes.
* Fit rules:
  * Possible prospects should have a visible facility buildout, modernization, ramp, or cleanroom/process-control need.
  * Possible prospects should plausibly need OT/IT integration, network/security infrastructure, real-time monitoring, cleanroom systems, process utilities, or commissioning support.
  * Primary fit is with facility owners, EPC/EPCM firms, cleanroom/facility contractors, and semiconductor manufacturing projects where OneSystems could support an integration or subcontractor role.
  * Do not treat large semiconductor companies as qualified by size alone; include them only when there is a visible project or operational trigger.

## 4. Exclusions and Caveats
* Excluded pure software/MES vendors, analytics platforms, facility-service competitors, recruiters, and generic semiconductor news with no facility trigger.
* Some listed prospects are very large and may be inaccessible directly. For megafabs, the practical path may be through EPCs, cleanroom contractors, facilities integrators, local construction managers, or approved supplier onboarding.
* Several candidates are broad facility owners rather than confirmed buyers for OneSystems. This file is a candidate pool, not a qualified outreach list.
* CHIPS-funded projects can shift schedule, scope, procurement routes, and contractor teams. Qualification should verify current construction phase, named EPCs/contractors, procurement contacts, and whether foreign SME participation is feasible.
